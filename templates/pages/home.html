Architecture snapshot Clean & Modular

Catalogue: products carry colour and size variants, each with its own SKU and price.

Taxonomy: one category per garment, plus brands, collections, materials and fit.

Accounts: email sign-in, verified email, addresses and activity history.

API: read-only catalogue endpoints under /api/v1/.

Frontend: Django Templates, Tailwind, HTMX, Alpine.js with a shared design system.
